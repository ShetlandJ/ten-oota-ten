How to get a 10
  1. Hang back: 2+ seconds behind him. No horn.
  2. Right indicator on for 3 seconds before you move out.
  3. Only go when it's clear: no oncoming, no bends, crests, solid lines, junctions or bus stops.
  4. Give him 1.5m (2m+ above 50mph). Don't crawl, don't blast.
  5. Left indicator on before you move back in.
  6. Move in only when he's well behind (a car length and more).
  7. Cancel your indicator. Nothing auto-cancels.
  8. Smooth hands, smooth feet, the whole way.

Touch: slide the steering bar, hold Gas/Brake (pressure builds), tap the arrows to indicate and tap again to cancel.
